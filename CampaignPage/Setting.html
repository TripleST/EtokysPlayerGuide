NPCs

This will describe all the various recurring non-player characters, mainly from the city of Sanctuary.

Bill
Bill is a sentient Ooze, formerly of the Gelatinous Cube variety—though he still identifies with them, despite being entirely unique. He is a large, bulbous figure who works as a hunter for the HG Alliance in Sanctuary, bringing in food through the use of his signature traps and ambush style of work. When he's relaxing, he can often be found at Giant Drinks. He has a beast companion, a Small spider/dog creature named Friend.

Ahmed Doohri
Ahmed is the Life Cleric who escorted the party through the dungeon and assured the party's survival in the Far Realm. In his life before the Far Realm, he worked as both a cleric and an adventurer, despite—or perhaps or because of—a curse placed upon him by the goddess of life who gave him his powers originally. Upon arrival in the Far Realm, he was nearly killed in the process of trying to protect the party; he barely survived, thanks to the aid of Geo, but he was left in a coma and has yet to wake. At the moment, he is staying with Gar and Nita in Sanctuary.

Gar
Gar is a lizardfolk Ranger who works for the HG Alliance. A fairly friendly individual, he finds the party in the wilds and guides them back to Sanctuary safely. He is skilled in tracking, hunting, and foraging, though he believes himself not patient enough for the trapping and ambushing style of work his coworker Bill specializes in. He is married to Nita.

Nita
Nita is an Eladrin who runs an apothecary in Sanctuary. Originally from the Feywild, she met Gar in the Far Realm and eventually got married. She can be mischievous but is usually willing to help people. Her skill in potion-making may prove useful to the party eventually.

Uggles (and Steve)
Uggles is the owner and bartender at Giant Drinks. He is a large ogre who is friends with Bill and has a pet owlbear named Steve, who also is the mascot for the tavern. In his occasional free time, he apparently frequents the library.

Riley (R'leigh)
Riley is a friendly illithid; librarian, author, and scholar, he spends much of his time at the library or seeking out new information to document. While his abilities in combat are unknown, he does have a solid grasp on his own psionic abilities, as well as a very basic understanding of magic from attending a few classes at a local wizarding school in Sanctuary. His name in Qualith is R'leigh, though he adopted the similar name of Riley to blend in easier in Sanctuary.

McAllister
The man known as McAllister is the proprietor of the HG Alliance—officially known as the Farager's Alliance, much to the chagrin of more practical individuals. He is a rather eccentric figure, seemingly impulsive and shortsighted, though he seems to be well-respected by those who work for him in spite of it.

Phobos
Standing atop a hill in the Harbor district is a mysterious figure named Phobos. Its mannerisms and speech are cryptic, its arrival in Sanctuary a mystery, and its abilities unknown. Despite how rarely people interact with it, Phobos does seem to care for the city, and it wishes happiness and safety for all the people in the city it overlooks from its hill.

Jeff and Diandra
Jeff is a human carpenter and woodworker who lives near the edge of the Harbor district. Married to (currently unknown to the party), he looks after their daughter Diandra, a playful half-orc child. The day that (REDACTED) was killed, Diandra was found lost near Giant Drinks. Geo found her and took her safely back to Jeff, who was staying at a neighbor's house after he got off work, since the police were actively investigating (REDACTED)'s death in their home.

The Wanderer
The Wanderer is a legendary figure in the Far Realm. For countless generations, illithids told tales of his battles and wanderings. One day, well after the establishment of Sanctuary, he encountered the city. Finally finding a place to call home, he retired to live a slower lifestyle. These days, he communicates exclusively through gestures and facial expressions and enjoys frequent cups of tea. Every now and then, he'll go for a walk away from the city, just to get a change of pace and admire the scenery now that he doesn't have to fight it to survive.

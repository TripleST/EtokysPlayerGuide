Homerules and Expectations


This is where you'll find the various rules for the campaign that might not be standard for core D&D. This might include various optional rules we'll be implementing, general rules regarding what is or isn't allowed during roleplay (because some people have boundaries), and other changes from core D&D rules (like adjustments to classes/features that I don't like lol). This section also includes more 4th-wall-breaking expectations, including establishing cancellation policies and whatnot. In other words, this is all the boring housekeeping stuff that tbh is probably unnecessary, but might get included for future reference.


Poisons

The rules for poisons in D&D can be somewhat... lacking. With a member of the party wanting to use poisons, I have elected to adjust some stuff about the poisons available in the game. The list of poisons available in the game, along with their rarities, types, gold cost (irrelevant for this campaign), and descriptions are as follows (ordered by rarity):
  • Assassin's Blood (150 GP) (Uncommon) (Ingested Poison)
    A creature subjected to Assassin's Blood makes a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 1d12 Poison damage and has the Poisoned condition for 24 hours. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and is not Poisoned.

  • Truth Serum (150 GP) (Uncommon) (Ingested Poison)
    A creature subjected to Truth Serum must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition for 1 hour. If the creature failed the saving throw, they cannot knowingly communicate a lie during that time frame, regardless of if they have the Poisoned condition. Being subjected to an effect that ends the Poisoned condition, such as one of the Restoration spells, also ends the truth-telling effect of the Truth Serum.

  • Torpor (600 GP) (Uncommon) (Ingested Poison)
    A creature subjected to Torpor poison must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition for 4d6 hours. On a failed save, the creature's Speed is also halved for the duration, even if it is immune to the Poisoned condition. Being subjected to an effect that ends the Poisoned condition, such as one of the Restoration spells, restores the creature's Speed to normal.

  • Lolth's Sting (200 GP) (Uncommon) (Injury Poison)
    A creature subjected to Lolth's Sting must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition for 1 hour. If the creature fails the save by 5 or more, the creature also has the Unconscious condition for the duration; a creature must still make a saving throw against the Unconscious condition, even if they are immune to the Poisoned condition. The creature wakes up if it takes damage or if another creature takes an action to shake it awake. Being subjected to an effect that ends the Poisoned condition also ends the Unconscious condition.

  • Serpent Venom (200 GP) (Uncommon) (Injury Poison)
    A creature subjected to Serpent Venom must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw, taking 3d6 Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

  • Malice (250 GP) (Rare) (Inhaled Poison)
    A creature subjected to Malice must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned and Blinded conditions for 1 hour. Being subjected to an effect that ends the Poisoned condition, such as one of the Restoration spells, also ends the Blinded condition.

  • Carrion Crawler Mucus (200 GP) (Rare) (Contact Poison)
    A creature subjected to Carrion Crawler Mucus must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. The creature also has the Paralyzed condition while Poisoned in this way. The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

  • Pale Tincture (250 GP) (Rare) (Ingested Poison)
    A creature subjected to Pale Tincture must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or take 1d6 Poison damage and have the Poisoned condition. The Poisoned creature repeats the save every 24 hours, taking 1d6 Poison damage on a failed save. The damage the poison deals can't be healed by any means while the creature remains Poisoned. After seven successful saves against the poison, the creature is no longer Poisoned.

  • Essence of Ether (300 GP) (Rare) (Inhaled Poison)
    A creature subjected to Essence of Ether must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned and Unconscious conditions for 8 hours. The creature wakes up if it takes damage or if another creature takes an action to shake it awake. Being subjected to an effect that ends the Poisoned condition also ends the Unconscious condition.

  • Burnt Othur Fumes (500 GP) (Rare) (Inhaled Poison)
    A creature subjected to Burnt Othur Fumes must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 3d6 Poison damage, and it must repeat the save at the start of each of its turns. On each successive failed save, the creature takes 1d6 Poison damage. After three successful saves, the poison ends. Being subjected to an effect that ends the Poisoned condition, such as one of the Restoration spells, also ends the lingering damage effect of this poison.

  • Oil of Taggit (400 GP) (Very Rare) (Contact Poison)
    A creature subjected to Oil of Taggit must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned and Unconscious conditions for 24 hours. It wakes up if it takes damage, or if it is subjected to an effect that ends the Poisoned condition, such as one of the Restoration spells.

  • Wyvern Poison (1,200 GP) (Very Rare) (Injury Poison)
    A creature subjected to Wyvern Poison makes a DC 14 Constitution saving throw, taking 7d6 Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

  • Midnight Tears (1,500 GP) (Very Rare) (Ingested Poison)
    A creature that ingests Midnight Tears suffers no effect until the stroke of midnight. Any effect that ends the Poisoned condition neutralizes this poison, though a creature immune to the Poisoned condition can still be affected by it. If the poison hasn't been neutralized before midnight, the creature makes a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 9d6 Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

  • Purple Worm Poison (2,000 GP) (Legendary) (Injury Poison)
    A creature subjected to Purple Worm Poison makes a DC 21 Constitution saving throw, taking 10d6 Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
